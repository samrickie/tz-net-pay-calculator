Understand the PAYE rates

PAYE is calculated in layers. Each part of your taxable income is charged according to the band it falls into.

TZS 0 – 270,000: No PAYE.
TZS 270,001 – 520,000: 8% of the amount above TZS 270,000.
TZS 520,001 – 760,000: TZS 20,000 + 20% of the amount above TZS 520,000.
TZS 760,001 – 1,000,000: TZS 68,000 + 25% of the amount above TZS 760,000.
Above TZS 1,000,000: TZS 128,000 + 30% of only the amount above TZS 1,000,000.

NSSF: default employee share 10%. PSSSF: employee share 5%.

HESLB: optional 15% of basic salary for an employed beneficiary.

WCF and SDL are employer costs, so they are not deducted from employee net pay here.
